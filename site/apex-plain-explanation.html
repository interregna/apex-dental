Apex Dental

Practice software.
Clear choices.

Apex Dental is free, open-source practice software: an independent fork of Open Dental (GPLv2) with a browser interface for scheduling, charting, billing, and reporting, plus voice dictation that runs on your own server by default.

This is a development preview, not a production or clinically validated system. Explore it with synthetic data. Its build instructions and security limitations are in the public repository.

What happens to dictation?

Local dictation keeps audio on your server. If you choose an external transcription service instead, audio is sent to that service. The screen identifies the configured engine before recording, and you review the transcript before filing it.

Would sharing data be required?

No. In the future, practices will be able to choose to share practice data with an optional external service under a separate, explicit data-sharing agreement. The goal is to provide aggregated fee and reimbursement benchmarks, insight into payer denials and payment delays, and better evidence for each practice’s own independent decisions.

Shared benchmark results are intended to be de-identified and aggregated. Data sharing is not available yet. It will be optional: using the software, or connecting it to any other service, never enrolls a practice in data sharing.

Where can I learn more?

Read the source, setup instructions, and current limitations on GitHub. This preview offers no production migration, hosting, availability, or reimbursement guarantees.
