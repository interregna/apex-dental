Apex Dental is free, open-source practice software: an independent fork of Open Dental (GPLv2) with a browser interface for scheduling, charting, billing, and reporting, plus voice dictation that runs on your own server by default.

Development preview. This is not a production or clinically validated system. Use synthetic data in an isolated development environment.

Practice workflows

Explore the scheduling, charting, billing, and reporting interfaces in the public source. Build instructions and current limitations are documented in the repository.

Local dictation

Local dictation keeps audio on your server. If you choose an external transcription service instead, audio is sent to that service. The screens identify the configured engine before recording.

Review before filing

Dictation produces an editable draft. Review and accept it before it reaches the chart. The preview still requires production security and clinical validation.

Optional, planned data sharing

More context for your own decisions

In the future, practices will be able to choose to share practice data with an optional external service under a separate, explicit data-sharing agreement. The goal is to provide aggregated fee and reimbursement benchmarks, insight into payer denials and payment delays, and better evidence for each practice’s own independent decisions.

Shared benchmark results are intended to be de-identified and aggregated. Data sharing is not available yet. It will be optional: using the software, or connecting it to any other service, never enrolls a practice in data sharing.
